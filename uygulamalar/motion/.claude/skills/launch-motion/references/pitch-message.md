# Pitch message — launch video proposal

Use on Upwork / Fiverr job posts for product launch videos. Open with their product, not with you: the client only sees the first two lines in the preview. Reuse the exact keywords from their post in those first lines.

Structure: their task → your concept → clear deadline, with a small first milestone so the client takes almost no risk.

```
Hi [Name],

Just went through [Product]. The [specific feature, e.g. "AI search across all your docs"] is the moment that will sell this in a launch video. Here's how I'd do 30 seconds:

0–5s: Hook. The problem in one line ("[pain point]"), bold kinetic type.
5–20s: Product in action. [Feature] animated straight from your UI, with fast camera moves and no fluff.
20–30s: Payoff. [Key benefit], your logo, and a clean CTA for [Product Hunt / your site].

How it works:
1. Storyboard and styleframes in [3–4] days, so you see exactly how it will look before any animation starts.
2. Full animation in [X] days, with 2 revision rounds.
3. Final delivery in 16:9, plus 9:16 and 1:1 cutdowns for socials.

Milestone 1 covers only the storyboard and styleframes, so you can judge the look with minimal risk.

Quick question: do you have the UI in Figma, or should we work from screenshots?

[Your name]
```

## Before you apply

- Client: payment verified, $10K+ spent, hire rate above 50%.
- Freshness: sort by newest, apply to posts less than 30 minutes old.
- Competition: under 15 proposals and nobody interviewed yet.
- Money: check their site; a freshly funded startup about to launch pays well and fast.
